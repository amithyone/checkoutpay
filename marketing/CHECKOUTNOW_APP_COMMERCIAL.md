# CheckoutNow App Commercial

**Campaign:** Use the app  
**Product:** CheckoutNow  
**Style:** MARKETING_BRIEF — calm, observational, lifestyle. Payments in the background. Commercial may invite action once. No hype.

**Primary CTA:** Download CheckoutNow / Open app → https://app.check-outnow.com  
**Secondary:** Same wallet on WhatsApp — message `WALLET`

**Tagline:** Your wallet, secured.  
**Promise line:** CheckoutNow moves money for life.

---

## Concept

**Title:** *Part of the day.*

Money moves. The phone stays quiet. No celebration. No “unlock your future.” Just the app, used.

**Tone:** Fashion-documentary meets everyday Nigeria. Slow. Dry. Confident.

**Visual mood (from brief):**
- Natural light, cafés, desks, taxis, kitchens — not neon fintech
- Phone in hand; UI visible but not screaming
- One person, or two people who already know each other
- Dark CheckoutNow UI is fine as product truth; frame it in calm real-world light

**Audio:** Ambient (traffic soft, café murmur, kettle). Soft music optional. No selling voiceover on the brand cut. Commercial cut may use one calm VO.

## Do / Don’t (this commercial)

**Do**
- Show real app flows (send, bills, ask)
- Keep pacing slow
- One clear CTA: open / download the app
- Mention WhatsApp parity once, not as the hero if this spot is app-first

**Don’t**
- “Cheapest”, “#1”, “trusted by millions”
- Emoji storms, confetti, countdown urgency
- Explaining why we’re better than other apps
- Stacking every feature in 15 seconds

---

## Production notes for director / editor

- Shoot like documenting a normal day. Payment is not the hero of the room — the person is.  
- If the payment moment disappears into the scene → correct.  
- UI: real CheckoutNow screens preferred over mock marketing chrome.  
- Music: soft, no drop. Silence is allowed.  
- End on the product name, not a speech.

**Internal line:**  
> We are not convincing. We are present. Then we say: open the app.
