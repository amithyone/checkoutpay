# CheckoutPay + CheckoutNow — Partner One-Pager

**Payments, simply.** · METRAVON INNOVATION LTD

---

## One line

CheckoutPay lets Nigerian businesses accept money. CheckoutNow lets Nigerians move, save, and spend that money — including paying merchants via WhatsApp Pay Code.

| Brand | Role | URL |
|-------|------|-----|
| **CheckoutPay** | Merchant gateway | https://check-outpay.com |
| **CheckoutNow** | Consumer wallet (app + WhatsApp) | https://app.check-outnow.com |
| **COPN** | WooCommerce plugin | WordPress.org |

---

## For merchants

- Bank-transfer checkout with **virtual accounts** + automated matching  
- **WhatsApp Pay Code** on the same checkout  
- Hosted checkout, REST API, webhooks  
- WooCommerce via **COPN**  
- Invoices, tickets, memberships, rentals, collections, payouts  

**Pricing:** **1% + ₦50** per successful transaction · No setup / monthly on standard pay-as-you-go  

| Amount | Fee |
|--------|-----|
| ₦10,000 | ₦150 |
| ₦50,000 | ₦550 |
| ₦100,000 | ₦1,050 |

**CTA:** Create a business account → https://check-outpay.com  

---

## For consumers

- Same wallet on **CheckoutNow app** and **WhatsApp** (`WALLET`)  
- Send / receive, bank transfer, Ask for money, Save Together  
- Bills: airtime, data, electricity, cable, betting  
- **USD virtual card** funded from NGN (Tier 2 KYC; **$7.50** setup)  

**CTA:** Download app → https://app.check-outnow.com · Or message **WALLET** on WhatsApp  

---

## Why this stack

1. Bank-transfer-first — not card-only  
2. Merchant pay-in and consumer wallet on **one platform**  
3. Transparent fees — state **1% + ₦50**, not “cheapest”  
4. Nigeria-focused: NGN, local banks, WooCommerce (COPN)
