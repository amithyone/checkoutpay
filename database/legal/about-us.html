Who we are

CheckoutPay is a Nigerian payment platform in its own right. We build reliable payment infrastructure for merchants, developers, and consumers — from hosted checkout and bank-transfer matching to WhatsApp Wallet, virtual accounts, and business modules you can turn on as you grow.

We work with trusted partners — including Metravon Innovation Ltd — for regulated safeguarding, settlement, and banking rails. CheckoutPay owns the product, merchant experience, and technology; our partners provide the licensed infrastructure behind the scenes. Our focus is simple: make it easy to get paid in Naira, keep fees transparent, and give you real-time visibility into every transaction.

Whether you run an online store, a rental shop, an event brand, or a SaaS product, CheckoutPay is designed to fit how Nigerian businesses actually operate.

Transparency

Security & trust

We apply controls appropriate to a financial platform — secure transport, access management, fraud awareness, and cooperation with regulators. Read our Security page for more.
